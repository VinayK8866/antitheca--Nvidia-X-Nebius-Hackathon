# 🎬 AntiTheca — Official 3-Minute Video Demo Script
### *Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon (Personal AI Track + $3,000 Tavily Sponsor Prize)*
**Target Length**: Strictly ≤ 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)  
**Speaking Pace**: 125–130 words/minute (~380 words total)  
**Setup Checklist Before Recording**:
1. Run `./start.sh` so both Backend (8000) and Frontend (3000) are running.
2. Have `http://localhost:3000` open in full screen on the **Console** tab.
3. Have a terminal window ready side-by-side or behind the browser for the 1:15 mark.
4. Have Discord open on another screen or phone if showing the live webhook embed.

## ⏱️ Video Choreography & Teleprompter Narration

### [0:00 - 0:25] ACT I: The Sycophancy Hook & The Core Problem
**Visual**:
- Split screen or rapid cut: Show ChatGPT's chat window for 5 seconds.
- User prompt on screen: *"I'm thinking of breaking my solo MVP into 20 microservices with Kafka..."*
- ChatGPT's response highlighted: *"Great idea! Here is a Kubernetes manifest..."*
- Cut to AntiTheca's sleek interface, flashing the contrast.

**Voiceover Narration (Spoken Word-for-Word)**:
> *"Every developer saves hundreds of technical architecture reels and Obsidian notes into a bookmark graveyard that rots without recall. Worse, modern AI assistants are dangerously sycophantic yes-men. If you tell ChatGPT you want to split a solo MVP into 20 microservices, it cheers you on and generates a Kubernetes manifest—completely ignoring that three weeks ago, you swore in Architecture Decision Record 028 never to do that."*

**Visual**:
- Switch to AntiTheca UI on the **Console** tab.
- Click the **"Reel / Slide"** upload button, or click **"Test Live Sample Slide: '20 Microservices Meme'"**.
- Show the image previewing. Click **"Ingest into Brain"**.
- Watch `Nemotron 3.5 Super-VL` parse it.
- **Immediate Action**: The red **Adversarial Radar** alert pill flashes in real-time. Click into the **Adversarial Radar** tab to reveal the contradiction card.

**Voiceover Narration (Spoken Word-for-Word)**:
> *"Meet AntiTheca: the first adversarial vault that protects your principles. Here, I ingest a viral architecture reel slide advocating for distributed Kafka microservices. Powered by NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super-VL on Nebius Token Factory, AntiTheca OCRs the diagrams and parses the technical claims in seconds. But instead of nodding along, AntiTheca's zero-sycophancy engine immediately sounds an alarm: flagging a direct, high-severity contradiction against our foundational ADR 028."*
